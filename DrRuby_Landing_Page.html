See the Difference

What dermoscopy actually reveals.

Two imaging modes. One device. A level of detail your naked eye — and your doctor's annual exam — will never capture.

Polarized vs. Non-Polarized Mode

Polarized light penetrates deeper into the dermis, revealing subsurface structures and vascular patterns invisible to standard imaging. Non-polarized captures surface texture and scale detail. Together they give a complete picture.

365nm UV Imaging

UV light illuminates pigmentation distribution, follicular structure, and fluorescence patterns at a microscopic level — biomarkers that directly correlate with biological skin age and cellular health.

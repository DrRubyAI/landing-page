How It Works

From skin scan
to real life wins.

Skin is the entry point — not the product. Your face reveals what's happening hormonally, metabolically, and systemically. We start there, and connect the dots.

01
"What's getting in your way?"
Not "what do you want to optimize." We ask what you want to do that your body isn't letting you do right now.
Run out of energy by 3pm Wake up exhausted Skin getting worse
02
Skin scan + baseline
A 60-second scan with your iPhone (or SkinScope attachment) captures three imaging biomarkers: inflammation, collagen proxy, pigment kinetics. This is your biological entry point.
03
Evidence-based experiment match
DrRuby matches you to a meaningful behavioral test — backed by science, not marketing. Some experiments are sponsor-supported: you may get the product free in exchange for real participation data.
04
Monthly: Is this helping?
We ask directly: is your tracking behavior bringing you closer to your goal — or has it become the goal? We coach you toward less dependency, not more.
